# YES-BANK-BBPS-Performance-Dashboard-Q1-FY-26-27-April-June-2026-.
## Project Objective:
Analyze the Q1 FY 26-27 (April – June 2026) performance data for YES BANK’s Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) BOU (Biller Operating Unit) & COU (Customer Operating Unit) integration. This analysis evaluates overall transaction volume, processed monetary value, participant performance (Aggregator Integrators/AIs and Billers), and month-on-month trends to provide strategic recommendations for driving transaction growth and ecosystem expansion in Q2 & Q3 FY 26-27.

## Note :
Due to data privacy and company confidentiality policies, raw datasets, customer records, and the original workbook are proprietary and cannot be shared publicly.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Data Analysis

- Total Transactions Processed: ₹43.5 Cr (435 Million) transactions   
- Total Monetary Value Processed: ₹11,792.99 Cr   
- Active Aggregator Integrators (AIs): 36   Active Billers: 37

## Key Dashboard Insights
- BOU Core Dominance: Biller Operating Unit (BOU) transactions account for the vast majority of total platform volumes (~98.4%), showing robust and increasing month-on-month velocity (from ₹12.82 Cr to ₹15.98 Cr).
- In-House Channel Leadership: Internal/proprietary entities lead both partner categories:
   - Yes Pay Next is the single largest AI partner, generating ₹0.07 Cr.
   - Yes Bank Credit Card is the top-performing biller, generating ₹0.54 Cr.
- High Utility & Microfinance Adoption: Apart from credit card collections, telecom (Jio Postpaid at ₹0.40 Cr) and microfinance/financial services (Belstar Microfinance at ₹0.07 Cr) are key drivers of biller transaction value.
- Partner Concentration: Top 2 billers (Yes Bank Credit Card & Jio Postpaid) constitute ~88.68% of the transaction value among top billers, highlighting a high dependency on major anchor merchants.

## Final Strategic Conclusion & Action Plan
- Scale Anchor Alliances & Expand Telecom/Utility Onboarding:Focus on onboarding high-frequency biller categories (Electricity, FASTag, Gas, and Tier-2/3 NBFC Microfinance institutions) mirroring the success of Jio Postpaid and Belstar Microfinance.
- Expand AI/Aggregator Distribution Channels:Strengthen partnerships with assisted-agent networks (like Nearby Technologies and MOS Utility) to drive deeper penetration into semi-urban and rural markets.
- Cross-Sell COU Integration via YES Pay Next:Drive marketing initiatives within the YES BANK mobile app/YES Pay Next to convert native bank customers into active COU bill-payers, balancing the current heavily BOU-dominant mix.
